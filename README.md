# Avants AI: Community Edition

This is the original version of Avants AI, a free tool that helps job applicants understand how their resume matches a job. I designed and coded this Community Edition myself, starting in July 2025.

**Live site:** https://resume-ns2m.onrender.com

## What it does

**Resume analysis:** upload a resume and get an AI summary of strengths and gaps.

**Job matching:** compare a resume against a job description and get a fit score with specific feedback.

**Audio summaries:** listen to your results read aloud with Google text-to-speech.

**PDF reports:** download your analysis as a PDF.

## Built with

Python, Flask, the OpenAI API, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, and Supabase, deployed on Render.

## Development history

The full history of how I built this, from the first commit on July 30, 2025 onward, is in the [commit log](https://github.com/Rhythmofthebeat/Resume/commits/main/).

## What came next

I later expanded Avants AI into a full hiring platform with job listings, AI voice interviews, and employer review tools, built by directing AI coding agents. That code lives in [Apricot](https://github.com/Rhythmofthebeat/Apricot) and runs at https://avants.ai. The Community Edition remains free.
